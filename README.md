# SkillsTrader Recruitment Management System

## About This System

This recruitment management system was developed specifically for SkillsTrader, an overseas manpower agency, to help streamline their internal recruitment processes.

The system was commissioned to assist SkillsTrader in managing their candidate sourcing, job placement, and recruitment workflow more efficiently.

## Purpose

This internal system helps SkillsTrader with:
- Managing candidate profiles and qualifications
- Tracking recruitment processes from application to placement
- Coordinating between different departments in the recruitment workflow
- Maintaining organized records of all recruitment activities
- Monitoring the progress of candidates through the overseas employment process

## Who Uses This System?

This system is used by:
- Recruitment coordinators and managers at SkillsTrader
- HR personnel responsible for candidate screening
- Administrative staff handling recruitment documentation
- Supervisors overseeing the recruitment process

## Main Features

### Candidate Management
- Store and organize candidate profiles and qualifications
- Track candidate progress through the recruitment pipeline
- Monitor candidate availability and readiness for overseas placement

### Job Management
- Create and manage job listings for overseas positions
- Track applications and candidate responses
- Coordinate with employers for job requirements and specifications

### Recruitment Tracking
- Monitor the status of candidates at each stage of recruitment
- Record communications and decisions throughout the process
- Generate reports on recruitment activities and outcomes

### International Employment Support
- Manage requirements for overseas positions
- Track visa and work permit documentation
- Coordinate between different time zones and locations

## Getting Started

### Accessing the System
1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
2. Navigate to the internal SkillsTrader application URL
3. Log in using your assigned credentials provided by the IT department

### Basic Navigation
Once logged in, you'll see:
- **Dashboard**: Overview of your recruitment activities and pending tasks
- **Candidates**: View and manage candidate profiles
- **Jobs**: Post and manage job listings
- **Reports**: View recruitment statistics and analytics

### User Roles
Different users have different access levels:
- **Administrators**: Full system access and management
- **Recruitment Managers**: Access to all recruitment features
- **Staff Members**: Limited access for specific tasks
