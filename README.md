# SkillsTrader

## What is SkillsTrader?

SkillsTrader is a tool designed to help manage recruitment processes. It's like a digital office assistant that helps companies find and hire the right people for their jobs.

Think of it as a smart system that organizes all the information about job seekers, employers, and job opportunities in one place. It makes the hiring process easier for everyone involved.

## Who Uses SkillsTrader?

SkillsTrader is used by:
- Hiring managers who need to find qualified candidates
- HR professionals who handle job applications
- Job seekers who want to apply for positions
- Company administrators who manage the system

## Main Features

### For Employers:
- Post job openings
- Review and manage job applications
- Track candidate progress through the hiring process
- Communicate with applicants

### For Job Seekers:
- Search and apply for jobs
- Track the status of their applications
- View job details and requirements

### For Administrators:
- Manage user accounts and permissions
- Monitor system activity
- Configure system settings

## Getting Started

### Accessing the System
1. Open your web browser (like Chrome, Firefox, or Edge)
2. Go to the SkillsTrader website address provided by your company
3. Enter your username and password to log in

### Basic Navigation
Once logged in, you'll see a dashboard with different sections:
- **Dashboard**: Overview of your activities
- **Jobs**: View and manage job postings
- **Candidates**: Review and manage job applicants
- **Reports**: See statistics about your hiring process

### User Roles
Different people have different levels of access:
- **Administrator**: Full access to all features
- **Manager**: Access to job and candidate management
- **Staff**: Limited access for specific tasks
